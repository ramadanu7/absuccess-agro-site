Our story

From a family farming business to a dependable supply company

Our story began in Ogbomosho, Oyo State, Nigeria, where our family started with local farming and agricultural production. Since 2017, that foundation has shaped our understanding of farmers, harvest seasons, commodity quality, buyer expectations, and the importance of reliable supply. Today, that local experience is growing into a structured agro supply company built to serve both domestic and international markets.

Over time, A & B Success Agro Ltd grew from local farming activity into a structured agro commodity supply business. We connect buyers with quality agricultural products sourced through trusted farms, aggregators, and suppliers across Nigeria, with a special focus on commodities such as cashew nuts, soybeans, ginger, hibiscus, beans, and other farm produce depending on season and buyer requirements.

Our growth is built on a simple belief: buyers need honesty, organization, and quality assurance before they commit. That is why we confirm availability, communicate sourcing timelines, inspect products, and provide realistic updates from the first inquiry to delivery readiness.
