Common questions about the products page

This page is designed to answer the first-level questions buyers usually have before they request a quote or ask for product-specific details.

Are these products available for immediate supply?

Availability depends on current stock, season, and order volume. Some products may be ready immediately, while others may require sourcing and preparation before confirmation.

What product details can buyers request first?

Buyers usually request specifications, sourcing locations, packaging, inspection status, quantity range, and expected lead time before moving to price and supply discussions.
