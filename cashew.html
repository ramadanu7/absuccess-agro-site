Market specifications

Raw Cashew Nuts product information

These are the key details buyers usually ask for before moving into pricing and supply confirmation.

Sourcing locations

  • Ogbomosho (Oyo State), Nigeria
  • Kwara State, Nigeria
  • Kogi State, Nigeria

Key specifications

  • Moisture Content: <= 8%
  • Nut Count: 180-220 nuts/kg
  • Outturn (KOR): 45-52 lbs
  • Foreign Matter: <= 0.5%
  • Defective Nuts: <= 10%

Quality and safety

  • Proper sun-drying
  • Free from mold and infestation
  • Cleaned and sorted
  • Stored in ventilated conditions

Packaging

  • Standard Packaging: 80kg jute bags
  • Use Fit: Export and industrial processing
