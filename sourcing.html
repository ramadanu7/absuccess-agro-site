Our sourcing approach

We combine local access with a structured supply process

Product availability can change because agricultural commodities depend on harvest periods, market movement, quantity requested, and current stock level. That is why we use a flexible sourcing system that allows us to support buyers even when a product is not immediately available in stock.

When a buyer sends a request, our team first confirms the product, quantity, grade or quality expectation, packaging needs, and destination. We then check current stock and determine whether the order can be supplied immediately or needs to be sourced through our trusted local network.

If sourcing is required, we provide a realistic timeline for sourcing, inspection, preparation, and delivery readiness before final confirmation. This helps buyers plan properly and reduces uncertainty in the buying process.

  • Stock confirmation before buyer commitment
  • Access to trusted farmers, local suppliers, and aggregators
  • Inspection and preparation before readiness confirmation
  • Clear 7-14 day estimated sourcing and preparation timeline

Step-by-step sourcing process

How we move from buyer request to supply readiness

Our process is designed to give buyers a simple and professional experience: confirm the request, check availability, source if needed, inspect, prepare, and communicate readiness.

Estimated Lead Time: 7-14 days
1

Buyer Request

The buyer shares the product, quantity, destination, quality expectation, packaging needs, and preferred timeline.

2

Stock Check

We confirm whether the requested product is available in stock or needs to be sourced from our network.

3

Local Sourcing

When needed, we source through trusted farms, local suppliers, and aggregators in key producing areas.

4

Inspection

We inspect product condition, handling, moisture level where applicable, and buyer specifications before readiness.

5

Preparation

Products are prepared for supply, packaging, loading, or export-readiness based on the buyer's requirement.

6

Timeline Update

We keep buyers updated on sourcing progress, inspection timing, preparation, and expected readiness.

7

Readiness Confirmation

Once the order is ready, we confirm the next step for delivery coordination or export documentation support.

8

Supply Support

We support buyer communication until the product is ready for movement according to agreed terms.
